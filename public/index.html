        Starter examples

        For Designers and Product people. You don’t need to write code.

        1. Start a page from a VA template. Under Pages, then Compose, choose New page and pick a VA page template.
        2. Build the page. Drop components onto the page. As you go, adjust the spacing and the semantics, such as heading order.
        3. Need a custom component or a different variation? Open the Components tab and describe what you need in plain words. Claude builds it from VA Design System parts, and you can refine it with a sentence.
        4. Need a form? Open the Forms tab and describe the form the same way. You can edit each page and field afterward.
        5. Save it, then use it. Save any new component or form to your library. It then shows up in the library on Compose, ready to drop onto your page.
        6. Get engineer sign-off. When you’re happy with the prototype, send it to your engineer under Review and export. They approve it or send back changes.
        7. Build your VA Prototype Kit. Once the engineer signs off, download the bundle from Review and export.

        Your work is saved only in this browser on this device. Nobody else can see it, so download or copy anything you need to share.
